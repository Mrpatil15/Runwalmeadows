F.A.Q

Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about Runwal Meadows, Kanjurmarg East.

Where is Runwal Meadows located?

Runwal Meadows is strategically located at Kanjurmarg East, Mumbai. It is situated right off the Eastern Express Highway and forms a key part of the expansive 36-acre Runwal City Centre township.

What apartment configurations are available and what is the starting price?

The project offers premium, spacious 2, 3, and 4 BHK luxurious apartments with private decks. The pricing for these air-conditioned homes starts from ₹1.89 Cr* onwards.

Is the project MahaRERA registered?

Yes, the project is fully registered under MahaRERA. The registration numbers are: Aster Tower (PR1181012502789), Daisy Tower (PR1181012502740), and Agent RERA (A51800003433).

What are the primary connectivity advantages?

Runwal Meadows offers superior connectivity: just 2 minutes to the Eastern Express Highway, 5 minutes to Kanjurmarg Railway Station, 10 minutes to Powai, and close proximity to the upcoming Metro Lines 4 & 6.

What kind of amenities does the project offer?

The project features state-of-the-art township amenities, including a swimming pool, clubhouse, fully-equipped gymnasium, sky gardens, kids' play areas, private deck spaces, and 24/7 multi-tier security.

How can I schedule a site visit?

You can easily book a free site visit by filling out any form on this landing page, clicking the "Book Free Site Visit" button, or directly reaching out to our property consultants via the floating WhatsApp or Call buttons.
